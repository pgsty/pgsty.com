Compare managed databases, cloud virtual machines and your own infrastructure against cost, reliability and operational responsibilities. PGSTY can help assess the requirements and plan a staged PostgreSQL migration.

## Historical estimates, with explicit limits

The [interactive calculator](/solutions/cloud-exit/#calculator) uses historical prices and fixed assumptions described in the [sources](/solutions/cloud-exit/#exit-sources). It is not live pricing, a quotation or a savings guarantee. Staffing, migration, networking, additional backups, taxes and risk costs are not fully included.

## A staged approach

1. **Assess.** Inventory workloads and dependencies, review actual spending, and identify operating responsibilities.
2. **Pilot.** Prepare a test environment and validate compatibility, performance, failover and recovery.
3. **Migrate.** Rehearse data movement, agree the cutover window, and prepare validation and rollback procedures.
4. **Operate.** Establish ownership of monitoring, changes, backups and incident handling, with any support arranged in writing.
