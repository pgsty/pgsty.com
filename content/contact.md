## Business enquiries

Email **[ron@pgsty.com](mailto:ron@pgsty.com)** for service enquiries, quotations,
partnerships, and procurement information. PGSTY PTE. LTD. is a Singapore
company providing software engineering and professional services for
PostgreSQL and open-source infrastructure.

We can help with:

- PostgreSQL architecture reviews, deployment, and migration planning.
- Database performance diagnosis, high availability, backup, and recovery.
- Pigsty implementation, observability, and technical support subscriptions.
- Engineering work involving the open-source projects we maintain.

## Tell us about your project

A useful first message includes your company name, the service you need,
your current database environment, the intended outcome, and your preferred
timeline. You can include a budget range or procurement requirements where
available.

Please start with a high-level description. Do not send passwords, private
keys, production database exports, or other sensitive customer data in an
initial email. If an engagement needs system access or confidential material,
we will agree an appropriate way to exchange it.

## From enquiry to delivery

1. **Requirements.** We discuss your environment, objectives, and constraints.
2. **Written proposal.** A quotation or scope of work sets out the deliverables,
   fees, schedule, responsibilities, and relevant service conditions.
3. **Agreement and invoicing.** The contracting entity and billing details are
   identified in the agreement and invoice before payment is requested.
4. **Delivery and review.** Engineering services are delivered remotely unless
   agreed otherwise. Work is reviewed against the agreed scope and acceptance
   criteria.

See [services and pricing](/price/) for the available plans, and
[website and service terms](/terms/) for information about delivery,
cancellation, and refunds. Project-specific terms are confirmed in writing
before work starts.

## Existing customers and project users

Customers should use the support channel agreed for their engagement.
For account or contract questions, email [ron@pgsty.com](mailto:ron@pgsty.com)
with your company name and the relevant quotation or invoice reference.

For public, non-confidential software questions and bug reports, use the
relevant repository in the [PGSTY GitHub organisation](https://github.com/pgsty).
Community issue trackers are public and do not replace a contracted support
channel. For a potentially sensitive security issue, contact us by email
before publishing technical details.
