## Website operator and scope

This website presents the software projects and professional services of
**PGSTY PTE. LTD.**, a Singapore company. Contact us at
[rh@vonng.com](mailto:rh@vonng.com) about these terms or a proposed engagement.

This page explains the website and our procurement process. The quotation,
order, scope of work, or service agreement accepted for a specific engagement
sets out its commercial terms. A website enquiry alone does not create a
paid service engagement.

## Services and quotations

Our commercial work includes PostgreSQL consulting, implementation, migration
planning, performance diagnosis, observability, and technical support for
Pigsty and related open-source infrastructure.

Published service descriptions and [prices](/price/) help you evaluate the
available options. The written quotation or order identifies the selected
service, currency, fees, applicable taxes, payment schedule, service period,
and any customer requirements. Confirm these details before accepting an
order or making payment.

Support coverage, response targets, project schedules, and acceptance
criteria apply only as specified for the selected service and in the
relevant written agreement. Public project resources do not by themselves
include a commercial support commitment.

## Ordering and payment

Start by sending a project enquiry through the [contact page](/contact/).
We discuss requirements and provide a written proposal where the work is a
fit. The contracting entity and payment instructions are stated in the
agreement or invoice.

This website does not offer a card checkout. Do not send payment card
details by email. If you receive changed payment instructions, verify them
through an established contact before paying.

## Delivery and acceptance

Professional services are delivered remotely unless the written agreement
specifies another arrangement. Depending on the scope, deliverables may
include technical advice, architecture recommendations, configuration work,
migration assistance, operational documentation, or access to an agreed
support channel.

The proposal or agreement defines the delivery schedule, required customer
access or input, and how work will be reviewed and accepted. Customers should
report a deliverable that does not match the agreed scope through the project
contact so it can be assessed and addressed under that agreement. A change in
scope, environment, or dependencies may require a revised schedule or quote.

Open-source software downloads are governed separately by the licenses and
release information provided with each project.

## Cancellation and refunds

To request cancellation, rescheduling, or a refund, email
[rh@vonng.com](mailto:rh@vonng.com) with your company name, quotation or invoice
reference, the service concerned, and the reason for the request.

Cancellation and refund eligibility depend on the accepted quotation or
service agreement, including the work already performed, any committed
costs, and the applicable service period. The proposal or agreement should
specify the applicable cancellation charges and how any refundable amount
is determined before payment is made.

If a service cannot be delivered as agreed, contact us to discuss the
appropriate next steps under the agreement, which may include a revised
delivery plan, cancellation, or a refund for the relevant undelivered work.
Applicable statutory rights are unaffected.

Access to customer systems, handling of confidential information, and any

provide only the access and information needed for the authorised work.
Changes to production systems, backup responsibilities, and operational
handover should be included in the agreed scope where relevant.
